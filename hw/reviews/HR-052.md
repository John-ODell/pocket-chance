# HR-052: Review of DR-052, baccarat layout and the clocked deal

- **Verdict: fits.** The redraw estimate is low by a third; nothing else moves.
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-04. Script `hwtest/baccarat_bench.py`: DR-052's geometry (Stud's bands, cards at x 56/100/144, three bet boxes at y 172–196 with labels and pays, prompt at y 202, DR-060's history squares, DR-055's rail) drawn with the real card primitives, real `lib/` (step 1n), 62.5 MHz SPI; code-drawn, then from a temporary card sheet (removed).

## Measured
| Path | Code-drawn | From a card sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Full redraw, six cards, boxes, prompt, history, rail + `show()` | **68 ms** (max 84) | **63 ms** (max 64) |
| Deal step: 72-row hand band with 1 / 2 / 3 cards + total line, pushed | 13 / 18 / 22 ms | 12 / 16 / 21 ms |
| Clocked deal P, B, P, B, 3rd, 3rd by the clock (`sleep_ms(300 − spent)`) | 300, 309, 297, 304, 300 ms spacing | 300, 304, 300, 304, 300 |
| Bottom band: boxes + prompt + history + rail, 72-row push | 12.0 ms (max 12.3) | 11.9 ms |
| RAM after the primitives (no baccarat module) | 58.4 KB in the harness | |

The dev's 50 ms for the full redraw misses the three boxes (three `fill_rect` + `rect` + two size-1 strings each ≈ 12 ms); the 26 ms band estimate is safe (22 ms with three cards).

## Limits
1. Full redraw only at deal start and result, as proposed; each dealt card is a band push (≤ 22 ms) inside its 300 ms slot, with time to spare for the total line.
2. Schedule by the clock as Stud and Hold'em do; the bench shows the spacing holds to ±9 ms.
3. The bottom band is cheap (12 ms) and can be pushed whenever the bet box selection changes; no need to split it.
4. Backs are not needed in baccarat (all cards face up), so the sheet case is a clean 5 ms gain per redraw.
5. Module RAM: by analogy with Stud (~15 KB, the dev's estimate is consistent), in-play ≈ 50 KB after collection on the step-1n build; confirm on the first upload with the usual mounted bench.

## Not measured
The baccarat module itself (not written); the help/pays screen (one `show()`, ~45 ms class).
