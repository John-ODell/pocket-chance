# HR-006: Review of DR-006, draft asset sizes

- **Verdict: fits**
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-03. Numbers from `hwtest/board_info.py`, `hwtest/ram_free.py`, `hwtest/flash_read.py`.

## Numbers
- **Flash today: 1,388,544 bytes free.** The dev's figure is confirmed: the board runs the stock Pico build and has a 1.4 MB filesystem (HR-F01). Phase 1 (434,816 bytes) plus slots (187,648) = 622,464 bytes **fits with 766 KB to spare**, before any firmware change. After a 16 MB firmware it is irrelevant.
- **RAM:** largest sprite (logo, 200x40 = 16,000 bytes) fits in a 16 KB scratch with 87.6 KB left after the framebuffer. A full-screen `table` (115,200) goes straight into the framebuffer, no extra RAM.
- **Load times:** card 40x56 (4,480 B) ~1 ms; chip 24x24 (1,152 B) ~0.3 ms; logo ~3 ms; table 20.5–26 ms. Layout arithmetic (eight overlapped cards in 236 px, two hands in 176 px) is geometry, not hardware; I have no quarrel with it.
- **Orientation** under `0x36 = 0x70` is **confirmed upright** (2026-10-04, `hwtest/byteorder.py`, John's visual check): (0,0) is the player's top-left with the board held landscape, joystick left, buttons right. Art is drawn the normal way up. See HR-003. The dev's pilot-first plan still stands and is the right gate for colours.

## What the dev must stay inside
- Keep the largest sprite at or under 16 KB so the single scratch buffer holds. The logo is at the limit; nothing bigger without a new review.
- Full-screen art only as `table`/`cabinet`, loaded into the framebuffer, never into a scratch.
