# HR-029: Review of DR-029, revealing the dealer's hand one card at a time

- **Verdict: fits.** Smooth with room to spare, including a garbage collection landing inside a gap.
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-04. Script `hwtest/stud_bench.py` (same setup as HR-026): four reveal steps, each redrawing the 72-row dealer band with one more card face up and pushing it, 300 ms apart, with a `gc.collect()` forced inside every gap to measure the worst pause.

## Measured
| Item | Code-drawn | Sheet cards |
|---|---|---|
| One reveal step: dealer band redraw (5 cards, hand line) + 72-row push | **25.8 ms** mean, 32.9 max | 28.4 ms, 30.3 max |
| `gc.collect()` inside a gap (blackjack-sized heap) | **11.5 ms**, 11.8 max | 11.3 ms |
| Worst step + worst pause | 44.7 ms | |
| Gap between flips | 300 ms | |
| Whole reveal: 4 steps + result redraw (~93 ms, HR-026) | ≈ 1.3 s | |

The dev estimated ~15 ms per step; it is ~26 because five cards sit in the band (four of them the dev's face-down backs, ~0.5 ms each drawn) plus the hand line and the felt. Still under a sixth of the gap.

## Reading
- Worst case inside a gap is 45 ms of work against 300 ms of waiting: **the flips land on time to within a frame and nobody will see a stutter**, even if the automatic collector runs between two flips. The 29 ms collection HR-F04 measured was with slots loaded; with blackjack-class scenes it is 11 ms.
- The reveal allocates nothing per step beyond the hand-name string, so the collector is unlikely to run at all during it; the measurement above forced it to prove the margin.

## Limits for the dev
1. **Schedule by the clock, not by `sleep_ms(300)`:** `sleep_ms(300 - spent)` with `spent` measured by `ticks_diff` around the redraw, so a slow step or a gc pause does not stretch the rhythm. (The slots spin did this; copy it.)
2. Redraw and push only the dealer band per step, as proposed; the result's full redraw comes once at the end.
3. Save after the result is drawn, as DR-007/HR-021 require; never between flips.
4. Drain `buttons.poll()` once after the reveal so presses made during it are dropped rather than queued.
5. Face-down cards code-drawn (HR-026 limit 1) keeps the step at ~26 ms once the art exists.

## Not measured
A flip animation (option C) was not benched; the dev rejected it and HR-F04 explains why per-frame animation is where the risk lives.
