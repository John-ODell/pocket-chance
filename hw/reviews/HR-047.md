# HR-047: Review of DR-047 (filed as DR-038), Ultimate Texas Hold'em reveal timing

- **Verdict: fits.** The same pattern as Stud's reveal (HR-029), with one extra rule inherited from HR-044.
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-04. Numbers from `hwtest/holdem_bench.py` (community and dealer band pushes with the real card primitives) and HR-029's forced-collection gap test.

## Measured
| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Flop: community band with 3 cards up, 60-row push | 27 ms |
| Turn + river together: community band with 5 cards up | 27 ms (max 32) |
| Dealer flip: dealer band with 1 then 2 cards up + 2 seat strips, 60-row push | 15 ms (max 17) |
| Result: full redraw, nine cards + strips | 85 ms drawn / 74 ms sheet (HR-043) |
| `gc.collect()` in this scene | 25 ms |
| Gap between dealer flips | 300 ms |
| Worst gap content: one flip + one collection | 40 ms of 300 |

## Reading
- Each turn of cards is a single band push well under 30 ms, so the 300 ms rhythm holds with room (HR-029 showed Stud's 26 ms steps land within a few ms of the target when scheduled by the clock). Flop and turn/river are single pushes each, not animations.
- The dev's "about 15 ms" for the community band is 27 ms with five cards; still irrelevant against 300 ms.
- Total pauses: flop pause 300 + two dealer flips 600 + result ≈ 1.0–1.3 s per hand, as the request says.

## Limits for the dev
1. **Schedule by the clock** (`sleep_ms(300 - spent)`), as Stud does.
2. **All six showdown evaluations and the seats' status strings before the first dealer flip** (HR-044 limit 1); gaps contain only a band push.
3. **`gc.collect()` before the reveal**; save after the result; drain the buttons after; nothing between flips (HR-021/HR-029 pattern).
4. Dealer cards face down code-drawn (HR-043 limit 1), so the flip bands stay at ~15 ms with art.

## Not measured
Nothing outstanding; the as-built screen will be benched like Stud's with `stage_bench`-style scripted keys.
