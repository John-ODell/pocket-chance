# HR-043: Review of DR-043 (filed as DR-034), Ultimate Texas Hold'em screen layout

- **Verdict: fits with limits.** The layout fits the screen and the budget; the redraw estimate is low by a third, and card art helps only where the cards are face up.
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-04. Script `hwtest/holdem_bench.py`: the request's exact geometry (dealer cards at x 76/120 y 28, community at x 12…188 y 88, player at x 76/120 y 148, four 60x56 seat boxes at x 8 and 172, 36 px bottom band of size-1 text) drawn with the real blackjack card primitives and real `lib/` (step 1j); code-drawn, then with temporary card and chip sheets (removed afterwards).

## Measured
| Path | Code-drawn | From sheets |
|---|---|---|
| **Full redraw, nine face-up cards + four text strips + bands + `show()`** (showdown / result) | **85 ms** (max 112) | **74 ms** (max 76) |
| Full redraw at the deal: two player cards up, seven backs | 49 ms | 67.5 ms (backs from the sheet are slower) |
| Community band push, 60 rows, 3 or 5 cards up (flop, river) | **27 ms** (max 32) | 26 ms |
| Dealer band push, 60 rows, 2 cards + 2 strips | 15 ms | 16 ms |
| One seat update via its row band (row redrawn + 60-row push) | 18 ms | 17 ms |
| Four text strips, drawing only | 2.7 ms | 2.7 ms |
| RAM free after the bench | 54 KB | 53 KB |

The dev estimated ~60 ms drawn / 55 ms sheet for the full redraw and ~15 ms for a band push. Where the full redraw goes: nine cards ≈ 35 ms, the push 18.5 ms, five felt bands and ~20 size-1 strings ≈ 20 ms, strips 2.7 ms, the one size-2 `$` figure ≈ 5 ms.

## Reading
1. **85 ms once per phase change** is the Stud scene's class (HR-026: 94 ms) and acceptable: it happens at deal, flop, river, showdown and result, never per key otherwise.
2. **The community band is 27 ms, not 15**: five cards in a 60-row band is 5 x 3.9 ms of cards before the 4.6 ms push. Still a fraction of the 300 ms pause around it.
3. **Face-down cards from the sheet cost 3.5 ms vs 0.5 drawn** (HR-026 limit 1 again): the deal scene has seven backs and got 18 ms slower with the sheet. Keep backs code-drawn.
4. The 36 px bottom band with size-1 text only is the right call: this screen has no room for size-2 text and the measurements show text is the second cost after cards.

## Limits for the dev
1. Face-down cards code-drawn always, even with art (or one `c_back` sprite held in RAM).
2. Full redraw only at deal, flop, river, showdown, result; bets and seat changes by band (15–18 ms measured).
3. No size-2 text beyond the chips figure.
4. The screen module will cost what Stud's does on import (~12–14 KB); with 78 KB at the menu that is fine. Measure on the first upload as usual.

## Not measured
The Hold'em screen module itself (not written). Banner art in the bottom band (160x32 from a sheet ≈ 2 ms).
