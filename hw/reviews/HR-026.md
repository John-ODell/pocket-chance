# HR-026: Review of DR-026, Caribbean Stud screen layout (ten cards in four bands)

- **Verdict: fits with limits.** The layout fits the screen and the budget; the dev's redraw estimate (50–60 ms) is about half the measured figure, and the card art does not speed this scene up.
- Reviewer: microcontroller expert, 2026-10-04. Script `hwtest/stud_bench.py`: DR-026's exact geometry drawn with the real blackjack card primitives (`Screen.card`, `card_back`, `felt`), real `lib/` (step 1i), `lib/poker.py` staged from the mount, 62.5 MHz SPI; once code-drawn, once with a temporary full `cards.565` sheet (removed afterwards).

## Measured
| Path | Code-drawn cards | From a card sheet |
|---|---|---|
| **Full redraw, 10 cards, 4 bands, hand names, result text + `show()`** (deal or result) | **93.6 ms** mean, 116 max | **91.4 ms**, 97 max |
| Raise or fold: dealer band (72 rows, 5 cards) + bottom band pushes | 22.4 ms | 32.7 ms |
| `poker.evaluate`, 5 cards | 1.0 ms | |
| `poker.evaluate`, 7 cards (for Hold'em later: 21 combinations) | 22.4 ms | |
| RAM for two five-card hands | 80 bytes | |

Where the ~93 ms goes: ten cards ≈ 39 ms (3.9 ms each), three size-2 strings (`$1000`, `ante 10`, `YOU WIN`) ≈ 21 ms at ~1 ms per character, the full push 18.5 ms, four felt bands and five size-1 lines ≈ 10 ms, two hand evaluations 2 ms.

## Reading
1. **93 ms per scene change** is a deal or a result, once per hand. At that rate the screen is a 10 fps scene, which is the floor the budget told the dev to design for, and it is a static card table, so it is acceptable. It is not the 50–60 ms the request assumed; the request forgot the text.
2. **The card art will not make this screen faster.** A face-up card from the sheet is 3.4–3.9 ms against 3.9 drawn (a wash), and a **face-down card from the sheet is ~3.5 ms against ~0.5 ms drawn**, which is why the raise/fold bands got slower with the sheet (four hole cards face down). DR-024's gain was measured on blackjack result scenes with no face-down cards.
3. The per-input paths (22–33 ms) are fine.

## Limits for the dev
1. **Keep face-down cards code-drawn even when the art exists**, or hold the one `c_back` sprite in RAM (4,480 bytes, a 1.5 ms blit). Either keeps the dealer band near 22 ms through the reveal.
2. **Size-2 text is ~1 ms per character.** Three short strings on a scene is 20 ms. Do not add more at size 2; prefer size 1 for the ante/raise detail.
3. Full redraw only on deal and result, as proposed. Everything else is bands (measured 22 ms).
4. Nothing in this layout needs new RAM: hands are 80 bytes; the screen module will cost about what blackjack's does (~14 KB on import). Fine against 78 KB at the menu.

## Not measured
The banner art (`banner_win` etc., 160x32 from a sheet ≈ 2 ms). The Stud screen module itself, not yet written.
