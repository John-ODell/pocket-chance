# DR-041 ruling: Caribbean Stud AI seats

- **Decision:** APPROVED as recommended
- **Approved by:** John, in chat with the PM
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Hardware review:** hw/reviews/HR-041.md (verdict: fits)

Five seats as chip stacks only, in a 12 px rail at the bottom of the Stud screen. Each seat is dealt five real cards from the same deck and plays the standard strategy against the same dealer hand, so results are honest and the player's odds are untouched. Green up or red down marker after each hand, cleared at the next deal. Seat count is a setting from 0 to 5 (default 5). Seats start at 1000 chips, refill silently, and are not saved. Measured: five seats decide in 5.3 ms, the rail draws and pushes in 3.1 ms, 496 bytes of state.
