# DR-033 ruling: Scope of the first custom board

- **Decision:** APPROVED, Option B (staged, two spins), with the owner's own answers below
- **Approved by:** John, in chat with the PM (answers to the one-at-a-time walkthrough, plus his follow-up messages)
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Hardware review:** HR-033, HR-P01, HR-P02 (all: fits)

## Spin 1 (what the owner chose)
1. **Chip:** RP2350A with 16 MB flash and 8 MB extra memory (PSRAM).
2. **Screen:** 1.54" 240 x 240 module (same pixel count as today, no layout changes).
3. **Wireless:** the Raspberry Pi RM2 radio module is **fitted** on spin 1.
4. **Sound:** the speaker amplifier and a speaker connector are **fitted** on spin 1 (no soldering for the owner).
5. **Extras the owner asked for:** microSD slot, real-time clock with a coin cell (DS3231-family, no charger on it), 6-axis motion sensor, STEMMA QT-style I2C connector, SWD header, test points. Temperature, humidity and GPS are connectors only.
6. **Battery and power:** a single lithium cell, either a LiPo through a connector or an 18650 holder (for now; the PCB maker chooses how to support both), **charged on the board**, and the board may run from USB while charging (power pass-through).
7. **Form:** one flat credit-card-style board, nothing stacked, to learn from and then shrink.
8. **Fab:** PCBWay. Everything small is assembled by the fab; the owner solders nothing.

## The owner's principle
Add wireless and the speaker first, see what happens, and remove them on the second board if they are not needed.

## Gate before any order
The design is run through KiCad's checks (ERC and DRC) and reviewed by the expert and a second review; the PCB maker prepares quotes; **the owner places the order**, from a checklist. Nothing is ordered without the owner's explicit yes.
