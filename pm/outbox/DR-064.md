- **Hardware review:** not needed
- **Drafted by:** senior dev, from John's chat rulings as relayed by the PM; the PM reviews before merge

See pm/inbox/DR-064-flop-game-table-multiplier.md for the full request. John's flop game (Casino Hold'em with a low/high call) takes the Caribbean row; Caribbean Stud is archived (John's three-games ruling).
x3 on the WHOLE payout of a winning hand (the Ante table pay and the call together), on the one hand that follows a group win. It lasts exactly one round, never stacks, is never saved or carried over, and only players who win that next hand get it; a hand that does not win gets nothing extra. Every player at the table, seats included, has the same chance. John first toned the app's xN down to 2x or 3x and then ruled x3 on the whole payout; the uncapped xN (option A, -61% of the Ante) is not built. Measured: about 5.2% of the Ante with x3 (x2 would be about 10%). Flag: the trigger counts a win only against a QUALIFIED dealer. John said "if all players beat the house"; since an unqualified dealer pushes every bet (DR-062), nobody beats the house on that hand, so the build arms the multiplier only when the player and every seat beat a qualified dealer. This reading was not put to John separately.
