# DR-020 ruling: Slots spin animation (revision 2)

- **Decision:** APPROVED as recommended (Option A, revision 2)
- **Approved by:** John, in chat with the PM
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Hardware review:** hw/reviews/HR-020.md (rev 1 did not fit; rev 2 fits with limits)

The reels scroll the real symbols and stop left to right 0.35 s apart. Built to HR-020's four limits: symbols held in RAM and read only when one scrolls in; reels staggered, at most one flash read per frame; compose pushed straight to the panel via LCD.show_buf; no stat, open, FrameBuffer or allocation in the frame loop. RAM choice A: two slots per reel plus the compose (about 43.9 KB, allocated on entry to slots, freed on exit). Fallback B (two shared slots, about 19 KB, 4 px per frame) if free RAM mid-spin is under about 20 KB; no new request needed for that. The first bench must report mem_free mid-spin.
