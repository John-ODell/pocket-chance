See pm/inbox/DR-059-baccarat-commission-rounding.md for the full request. Baccarat is the owner's next game (D-019). Standard punto banco, 8 decks, Player 1:1, Banker 1:1 less 5% commission, Tie 8:1.
The Banker commission is rounded UP to whole chips (the winner is paid stake*19//20). Owner chose this over Banker stakes in multiples of 20 and over a no-commission variant. Known cost: about 7.9% house edge at a stake of 5 and 3.4% at 10; exactly 1.06% at 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100.
