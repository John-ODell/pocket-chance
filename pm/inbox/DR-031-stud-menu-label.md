## The decision
The word on the menu row for Caribbean Stud. The row must be at most 9 characters at size-2 text next to the icon slot (DR-022 ruled "Carib. Stud", which does not fit; the row currently reads "Caribbean").

## Options
### A. "Caribbean" (recommended)
- Pros: the game's own name, already on the board.
### B. "Stud"
- Pros: short; some players know it as Stud Poker.
- Cons: less specific.
### C. "Carib Stud" (10 characters)
- Cons: does not fit at size 2 (160 px against 152 available).

## Recommendation
Option A. Inside the game the title line says "Caribbean Stud".
