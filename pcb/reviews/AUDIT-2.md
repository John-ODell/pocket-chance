# Independent PCB audit 2

Date: 2026-10-04. Checkout: `<repo>`, branch `main`.

**Disposition: do not fabricate or connect a cell on the strength of this design yet.** This is an audit of the populated, 124-component schematic. The most serious findings are the reverse-polarity guard topology and the speaker amplifier package mismatch. The permanently connected battery ADC divider, charging safeguards, joystick mapping and unfinished footprints also need resolution.

Severity: **BLOCKER** = could damage hardware or prevents the intended hardware from working; **MAJOR** = likely wrong or a substantial unresolved implementation/safety issue; **MINOR** = limited defect or improvement; **NOTE** = observation or verification limitation. A severity assigned to an evidence gap is not proof that an unverified part is electrically wrong.

## Numbered independent findings

1. **BLOCKER — Q2's body-diode direction does not provide the claimed reverse-battery isolation.** References: **Q2, R22, J3, U5, U7**. Nets: **CELL_P, CELL_G, BAT+, BAT-, PROT_VCC**. The netlist has Q2 gate pin 1 on BAT-, source pin 2 on CELL_P (J3 pin 1), and drain pin 3 on CELL_G, connected through the fitted 0-ohm R22 to BAT+. In the drawn P-channel symbol the intrinsic diode conducts **from drain to source**, hence BAT+ toward CELL_P. With a reversed cell, CELL_P is the cell's negative terminal and the gate is connected to its positive terminal; switching the channel off does not remove this diode path. In particular, USB-powered U5 can drive the BAT+ side while the reversed cell is still connected to that diode. U7 also senses the guarded BAT+ through R20, rather than being isolated from a possible negative rail. This is an unsafe topology to assume tolerant of a backwards battery. The precise full fault current depends on Q1/U7 state and their internal paths, which cannot be certified without their missing datasheets; damage is a risk, not a claimed measured outcome. **Source:** AO3401A PDF p.1 confirms the symbol's body diode; BQ2407x PDF pp.8, 10 establish BAT connection/ratings; application reasoning **from general practice, unverified** for the actual DMG2305UX, whose datasheet is absent. **Suggested fix:** redesign and document the reverse-polarity protection, including diode orientation, gate bias, low-side protector behavior and charger-powered fault paths. A simple discharge-only guard is not sufficient evidence for a bidirectional charging circuit. Verify correct/reversed/no cell with USB present/absent using exact-part documents before any real-cell test.

2. **MAJOR — Battery voltage feeds a non-fault-tolerant ADC when the board is switched off.** References: **R23, R24, C28, U1 pin 42, SW3, U6, J8 pin 6**. Nets: **BAT+, VBAT_SENSE, +3V3, PWR_EN**. R23 and R24 are 100 kOhm each, permanently dividing BAT+ to approximately half its voltage. SW3 disables U6, but leaves this divider connected to GPIO28/ADC2. With +3V3/IOVDD at zero and a normally connected charged cell, the divider attempts to put about 2.1 V on a standard ADC IO. The RP2350's powered-off digital-pin tolerance does not apply to this ADC pin: ESD-diode leakage/back-power is explicitly described, and the standard-IO limit is IOVDD + 0.5 V. The resistors limit current, so permanent damage is not proven, but unintended partial powering and operation outside the documented input limit remain. **Source:** RP2350 Datasheet PDF pp.402, 1332-1333, 1336; Pico Datasheet schematic PDF p.26 explicitly uses a transistor to prevent this type of off-state ADC leakage. **Suggested fix:** switch/isolate the battery measurement when IOVDD is absent using a verified topology, or otherwise meet the datasheet limits in every power state. Document J8 pin 6 as a battery-sense ADC node, not unrestricted GPIO.

3. **BLOCKER — The selected amplifier will not fit the assigned package.** Reference: **U8**. Nets: **+3V3, GND, AUDIO_INP, AUDIO_INN, SPK+, SPK-**. Value is PAM8302AASCR, but footprint is `Package_SO:SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm`. The exact AASCR ordering code is **MSOP-8**, not SO-8. The symbol description also says MSOP-8. An assembly made from this MPN and footprint cannot reliably connect the intended chip. **Source:** PAM8302A PDF p.9 ordering table and pp.10-12 package/land drawings. **Suggested fix:** use the verified MSOP-8 footprint for AASCR, or explicitly select the SO-8 variant from the ordering table and recheck its package, symbol and BOM. Do not substitute silently.

4. **MAJOR — One physical joystick is represented as five unrelated two-pad packages.** References: **SW8, SW9, SW10, SW11, SW12**. Nets: **JOY_UP, JOY_DOWN, JOY_LEFT, JOY_RIGHT, JOY_PRESS, GND**. Each is a separate `SW_Push` component with signal on pin 1 and ground on pin 2, all assigned the same full `pcb_custom:Alps_SKRHABE010` joystick footprint. There is no single physical component reference or distinct real-contact pad mapping. Creating that footprint alone would still cause five joystick instances to be transferred to the PCB; superimposing them would create conflicting pads/nets. **Source:** native schematic/netlist and KiCad component/package semantics; **general practice, unverified** against the absent Alps datasheet. **Suggested fix:** model the actual joystick once, with all contacts mapped to its real pads, using one multi-unit component or one complete symbol and one footprint. Verify direction/contact naming and common terminals from the actual Alps drawing; no pad numbers are assumed here.

5. **MAJOR — Seven assigned custom footprints cannot be resolved.** References: **SW8-SW12, U10, U11**. Nets: the five **JOY_*** nets, **SDA/SCL/IMU_INT/+3V3/GND**, and **WL_*** nets. Assigned footprints are `pcb_custom:Alps_SKRHABE010`, `pcb_custom:ST_LGA-14_2.5x3mm` and `pcb_custom:RM2`; the referenced `lib/pcb_custom.pretty` is not supplied. Both ERC reports identify these seven components. **Source:** supplied/fresh ERC and `fp-lib-table`; RM2 Datasheet PDF pp.9-11 supplies its required footprint/numbering. **Suggested fix:** supply verified custom land patterns, including RM2's metal-free antenna area, and resolve the joystick model in finding 4 before transferring the circuit to a PCB. This is a fabrication blocker until resolved, not proof that the schematic signal names are wrong.

6. **MAJOR — Core inductor footprint and orientation requirements are not carried through.** Reference: **L1**. Nets: **VREG_LX, 1V1**. Value specifies AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T, but the assigned footprint is generic **0805/2012**, whereas Raspberry Pi specifies **0806/2016**, a 2.0 x 1.6 mm polarity-marked part. The installed footprint has an unmarked symmetric fabrication outline and no winding-orientation indicator. Its pad fit has not been established against the exact special part. The local Abracon family document lists other inductance codes and says “No Marking”; it does not establish the special 3R3-101-T variant's drawing or winding orientation. The regulator is sensitive to that orientation, not just inductance. **Source:** RP2350 Datasheet PDF pp.412-414; Hardware design with RP2350 PDF pp.7-8; AOTA-B201610S family PDF pp.1, 3; assigned footprint inspected directly. **Suggested fix:** validate a footprint against the exact part, mark the orientation unambiguously in fabrication/assembly information, and follow the regulator layout/current-loop recommendations. The present observations do not prove the generic pads could never be soldered, but they are insufficient for this critical regulator.

7. **MAJOR — A safe charge-current setting has not been established for an identified cell.** References: **U5, R14, R17, J3**. Nets: **ISET, ITERM, BAT+**. R14 is 1.8 kOhm, giving a programmed nominal fast-charge target of **890/1800 = 0.494 A**, with the datasheet factor range approximately **0.443-0.542 A** before source/load/thermal limits. R17 is 1.5 kOhm, giving nominal termination **0.030 x 1500/1800 = 0.025 A** in USB500 mode. No exact cell type, allowed charging current or temperature range is established by the circuit. A small cell cannot be assumed to tolerate this programmed target. The input current limit may reduce actual charging, but is not a substitute for choosing the correct cell charging rate. **Source:** BQ2407x PDF pp.13, 34; Pico 2 W PDF p.17 warns that the cell's permitted current/temperature range must be respected. **Suggested fix:** tie charging voltage/current, precharge, termination and timer settings to a specified cell's official limits; record resistor tolerances and worst cases. I do not prescribe a replacement resistor without that cell specification.

8. **MAJOR — The cell's temperature is not monitored during charging.** References: **U5 pin 1, R12, J3**. Net: **TS**. R12 is a fixed 10 kOhm to GND; J3 has only the two power contacts. This is a valid TI method for bypassing the temperature check, but it permanently presents an acceptable temperature indication and cannot stop charging an excessively hot or cold cell. U5's internal thermal regulation protects its own junction, not the cell's temperature. **Source:** BQ2407x PDF pp.9, 29, 35; Pico 2 W PDF p.17. **Suggested fix:** provide the appropriate pack/cell temperature sensing or another documented means of enforcing the chosen cell's charging-temperature limits. If bypass is intentional, explicitly document the remaining limitation and do not describe the circuit as having cell-temperature protection.

9. **MAJOR, conditional on closing JP2 — The shared interrupt lacks a verified safe drive mode and pull-up.** References: **U10 pin 4, U9 pin 3, JP2, U1 pin 18**. Nets: **IMU_INT, RTC_INT**. JP2 is open by default, so there is no presently connected output-to-output short. Closing it joins the symbol's IMU INT1 output to the RTC open-collector interrupt. No external pull-up exists on either interrupt net. If the IMU drives high while the RTC pulls low, contention can result; if both are open drain, an appropriate pull-up is needed. Neither the LSM6DSOX nor DS3231M exact datasheet is present, so actual reset behavior, configurable output modes and backup-supply interrupt behavior cannot be certified. **Source:** actual netlist/symbol types; electrical sharing requirements **from general practice, unverified** for these exact ICs. **Suggested fix:** verify both devices' exact output modes/defaults and interrupt polarity; add a suitable pull-up and isolation if needed, or keep separate interrupt signals. Document safe JP2 configurations and firmware initialization. Do not assume a firmware setting persists across reset.

10. **MAJOR — L2 is a nominal value, not a fully specified power component.** References: **L2, U6, C23-C26**. Nets: **BB_L1, BB_L2, VSYS, +3V3**. L2 is only “2.2uH” with a generic footprint: no selected MPN, saturation/rated current, resistance or tolerance. These are required to judge boost operation at the lowest battery voltage and simultaneous MCU/radio/display/audio/card load. No transient load budget is established by the schematic. C23 is 10 uF and C25/C26 total 20 uF nominal, which are sensible nominal input/output amounts, but effective capacitance and voltage ratings are unspecified. **Source:** TPS6300x PDF pp.11-12 gives selection procedure and saturation margin; p.16 requires close placement; **general practice, unverified** for the eventual selected L2/capacitors. **Suggested fix:** select and document exact inductor/capacitor parts against minimum input voltage, peak current, heating and voltage-bias derating, then verify layout and load transients. A footprint size alone does not specify current capability.

11. **MINOR — The latest RP2350 datasheet calls for additional remote DVDD capacitance.** References: **U1, C8-C10, C14**. Net: **1V1**. There are three 100 nF DVDD capacitors and only one 4.7 uF on the core output. That matches the minimal-design guide's decoupling figure, but the RP2350 Datasheet also specifies a 4.7 uF near the distant DVDD supply and recommends a second 4.7 uF away from LX/COUT, specifically near QFN-60 DVDD pin 23. One capacitor cannot occupy both separated locations. **Source:** RP2350 Datasheet PDF pp.401, 413; Hardware design with RP2350 PDF p.9 shows the simpler three-100-nF pattern. **Suggested fix:** reconcile the two official descriptions and provide the extra remote 4.7 uF/local placement per the full datasheet. Nominal total capacitance alone does not settle its location. This is not a claim that the minimal example necessarily fails.

12. **MINOR — Audio power-on mute and recommended local high-frequency supply bypass are not explicit.** References: **U8 pin 1, C32**. Net: **+3V3**. Shutdown is tied directly high, so the amplifier enables with its supply and cannot be muted during PWM initialization. C32 supplies 10 uF, but there is no identified local 1 uF amplifier supply capacitor (C30/C31 are input-coupling parts). Datasheet recommendations are a delayed shutdown release and local 1 uF plus 10 uF supply decoupling. **Source:** PAM8302A PDF p.8. **Suggested fix:** implement a suitable startup mute/shutdown strategy and the recommended local bypass. Check speaker impedance/power and cable EMI separately. This affects pop/noise/robustness; it is secondary to finding 3.

13. **NOTE — R15 does not set the current limit in the wired input mode.** References: **U5 pins 5/6/12, R13, R15**. Nets: **CHG_EN1, ILIM, VBUS, GND**. EN2 is grounded; EN1 is pulled to VBUS through 10 kOhm. With USB present this selects **USB500**, not the resistor-programmed ILIM mode. R15's 3.0 kOhm is connected but does not define the active input current limit in this configuration. **Source:** BQ2407x PDF pp.8-9, 12. **Suggested fix:** document USB500 as the selected mode, or wire/control the intended alternative deliberately. Check permitted source current and USB enumeration/Type-C current behavior separately; USB compliance is **general practice, unverified** here because its specification is not among the supplied references. Do not budget simultaneous loads using a supposed R15-programmed limit.

14. **NOTE — U10's symbol and Q2's symbol name do not match their selected part values.** References: **U10, Q2**. Nets: **SDA, SCL, IMU_INT, +3V3, GND**, and **CELL_P/CELL_G/BAT-**. U10 uses an LSM6DSM symbol with value LSM6DSOXTR; Q2 uses an AO3401A symbol with value DMG2305UX. Such reuse can be valid, but there is no exact-part datasheet here to certify pin equivalence, limits or defaults. The U10 SA0-to-ground ERC warning is consistent with selecting I2C addressing rather than an actual power-output short, but only the correct device document can confirm it. **Source:** native symbol/netlist identities; substitution verification **from general practice, unverified**. **Suggested fix:** attach exact-part pin/behavior comparisons or use verified exact symbols. Do not resolve the warning merely by changing the symbol's electrical type without verifying the selected part.

## Could NOT verify, and why

- **Actual battery safety:** no exact cell datasheet and no DW01A, FS8205A or DMG2305UX datasheets. Protection thresholds/delays, FET pad equivalence/current capability, recovery and complete wrong-polarity current paths remain unverified. The AO3401A document cannot certify a differently named fitted FET. Charger temperature bypass and guard findings are not cured by an ERC pass.
- **PSRAM:** exact AP Memory APS6404L-3SQR-SN datasheet absent. The MCU supports the intended architecture, but U3's exact pinout/package/voltage/timing and firmware configuration are unverified.
- **RTC / IMU:** DS3231MZ+ and LSM6DSOXTR official documents absent. I cannot certify their addresses/address conflicts, unused-pin treatment, substitute-symbol equivalence, shared-interrupt output modes, backup behavior or IMU supply specifications. No numeric I2C addresses are invented here.
- **Display / backlight:** exact 1.54-inch module source absent. J4's pin order, JST cable mapping and backlight input type/current are unverified. A raw LED backlight must not be assumed safe to drive directly from GPIO13. The existing 1.3-inch HAT measurements do not verify this module.
- **ESD / connectors / switches:** USBLC6-2SC6, Molex 104031-0811, Alps SKRHABE010, PTS125 game buttons, PTS645 boot/reset buttons and PCM12 power-switch exact sources are absent. Standard library names are not independent manufacturer validation. JST PH/SH family PDFs are available but do not verify a particular assembled cable's polarity or every assigned land pattern. Speaker impedance/power and connector confusion with the same two-pin battery socket also require physical verification.
- **Coin-cell holder:** the supplied `keystone-3001.pdf` is not a verified exact 3001/CR1220 land-pattern source. The assigned 3001 footprint and stated CR1220 compatibility remain unchecked; no alternate holder size is guessed.
- **Core inductor:** supplied Abracon family document does not cover the special marked 3R3-101-T variant. Raspberry Pi names it and specifies required characteristics, but its exact terminal/land drawing and winding orientation must be established before placement.
- **Passive specifications:** nominal values and package sizes alone do not establish capacitor voltage, dielectric, DC-bias derating, ESR/ESL, resistor tolerances or L2 ratings. C13/C14/C15 in particular must meet the RP2350 regulator requirements, not merely say 4.7 uF.
- **Layout:** PCB file has no implemented layout. Therefore no antenna keep-out, ground plane, component placement, USB impedance, crystal trace parasitics, QSPI/PSRAM bus loading, supply-loop geometry, feedback routing, thermal copper, current capacity or decoupling proximity was passed. RM2 PDF pp.9-10 requires a minimum metal-free antenna region; p.12 requires proper grounding away from that region. RP2350 PDF pp.410-414 makes core regulator geometry/orientation critical.
- **Firmware / power states / measurements:** no hardware exercised or firmware loaded. PSRAM initialization, safe interrupt mode, USB enumeration/current policy, backlight drive, screen orientation/init, radio startup, sleep/wake, charger heat, battery runtime and every first-power-up transient remain untested. RM2 GPIO29 is used only as a digital clock here; there is no Pico-style VSYS measurement divider on it.
- **PDF/source version identity:** enlarged PDF blocks agree with the native connection/values sampled, and historical/fresh ERC counts agree. I did not establish a cryptographic source-to-PDF build identity. The native netlist is authoritative for the connections cited.

Additional findings discovered by comparison (numbering continues):

15. **MINOR — Charger “28 V OVP” claim is incorrect.** Reference: **U5**, also **U4/C20** if used to imply whole-input tolerance. Net: **VBUS**.
