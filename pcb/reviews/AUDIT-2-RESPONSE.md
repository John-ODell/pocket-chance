## Revision 0.5 (after the third audit pass's first findings, 2026-10-04)
Three findings from AUDIT-3 and the expert's HR-P05 part 4, all accepted:
- **AUDIT-3 #2, ILIM:** my rev 0.3 marked R15 not fitted; the BQ24074 pin table says "Leaving ILIM unconnected disables all charging". R15 (3.0 kΩ) is fitted (commit daa722b). Charge-rate wording corrected: in USB500 the 500 mA total input bounds a 1000 mAh+ cell to at most 0.50 C; a 500 mAh cell with 3.6 kΩ is ISET-bound at up to 271 mA (0.54 C).
- **AUDIT-3 #1, reversed cell with USB present:** my rev 0.3 isolation argument held only without USB; with USB the charger powers the DW01A, Q1 holds BAT- at ground, and a reversed cell turned Q2 on. Rev 0.5 replaces the BAT- gate reference with a polarity sensor: R45 100 kΩ holds Q2 off; Q5 (AO3400A, Vgs(th) 0.65 to 1.45 V, ao3400a.pdf) pulls Q2's gate down only while the connector's + pin is positive (R46 10 kΩ, R47 47 kΩ); R48 1 MΩ is a bleed. **Correction after the auditor's ON-state trace (AUDIT-3): cell removal with USB present does NOT release the guard**; the charger holds the connector pin at BAT+ through Q2's channel and Q5 stays on, so the socket stays live until USB is unplugged, and a reversed cell inserted in that window meets an ON guard (bounded to a charger-limited forced discharge until the cell's PCM trips, no reverse charging; still a fault). The earlier 'dead socket' and 'tripped pack woken' claims are withdrawn. No passive two-wire interlock exists; mitigation is the owner rule 'unplug USB before changing the battery', and whether the charger's own battery-detection dip releases the latch is to be measured at bring-up. A stronger interlock is a decision for John (PCB-002). Standing drain with a cell fitted and the board off: about 110 µA (BATTERY.md).
- **AUDIT-3 #3, RM2 land pattern:** bottom row was +0.5 mm in x and the side columns +1.0 mm in y. Corrected per Figure 6 (side pads ±6.75, y = -2.75 + 1.5k, 2.0 x 1.0; bottom pads x = -4.50 + 1.5k, y = 7.85, 1.0 x 1.8), courtyard now 0.25 mm beyond the pads, and the RF keep-out is written into the footprint as a rule area on both copper layers as well as a drawing (enforcement to be confirmed at layout; a board-level rule area will be added there regardless).

## Revision 0.7 (John's option D, 2026-10-04)
Added F1, a resettable polyfuse in the cell's positive lead (between the socket pin and the ammeter link; charging and discharging both pass through it, USB does not): Bourns MF-MSMF110/16X-2, 1.10 A hold (0.77 A at 60 °C), 2.20 A trip, 16 V, 1812, about 0.3 USD (estimate). **It contains a shorted lead, a failed protection transistor or a board short, and limits the heat after a wiring mistake. It does NOT save the two chips a reversed cell destroys** (the DW01A and the charger's BAT pin). It costs a few tens of millivolts in the battery lead, so charging ends a few minutes later than it otherwise would. R20 changed from 0402 to 0603 so it survives the ~36 mA of a reversed cell with USB absent (HR-P05 part 7). Nothing else changed from rev 0.6.

Expert's rev 0.7 verification (HR-P05 part 8): exactly F1 and the R20 package differ from rev 0.6; placement, termination effect (CV runs minutes longer, no safety effect), derated hold vs the board's draw (0.27 A margin at 60 °C) and fault behaviour confirmed. Two non-blocking notes carried forward: BATTERY.md now says a tripped fuse resets only after the cell is unplugged; R20 at 100 Ω 0603 still dissipates about 30 % over its rating in the USB-absent reversed-cell fault, so the next revision changes it to 330 Ω (or an 0805) once the DW01A datasheet confirms its supply-filter range. Not changed now, to keep rev 0.7 the review point.
